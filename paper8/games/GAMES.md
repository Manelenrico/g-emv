# The complete games

The complete games of the sealed experiments are not here because of their size: 11,720 games, 85,080 files, 38.2 GB. They are available from the author on request. `games_manifest.csv` gives, for every file of every game, the seal, the folder, the game, the file, its size in bytes and its md5, so that anyone who holds them can check they are the same. Seal 4 asked the creatures questions and played no games.
